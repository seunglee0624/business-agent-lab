## 2. Findings

1. **new-arrivals fell on every measure.** Clicks dropped from 900 to 480 while impressions rose from 30,000 to 32,000, so CTR halved. Purchases fell from 18 to 8, so cost per purchase rose from $30 to $70 and ROAS fell from 2.33 to 1.00.
   - Did the ad creative, the targeting, or the placement change around 5 October?
   - Is the ad still showing the right products, or did those products sell out?
   - Did tracking or the landing page break, so that fewer clicks and purchases were recorded?
2. **retargeting got better with the same spend.** Spend stayed at $600, but purchases rose from 20 to 24 and revenue rose from $1,800 to $2,400. Conversion rate rose 0.63 pp and ROAS rose from 3.00 to 4.00.
   - Did the audience change? For example, did shoppers from autumn-sale or new-arrivals get added to it?
   - Did average order value go up because of a promotion or a change in the product mix?
3. **The other two campaigns held steady.** autumn-sale and brand-search changed by less than 0.1 pp on CTR and conversion rate. This suggests the new-arrivals drop is limited to that one campaign and is not a site-wide problem.
   - Can we confirm that by checking site-wide traffic and checkout data for that week?
